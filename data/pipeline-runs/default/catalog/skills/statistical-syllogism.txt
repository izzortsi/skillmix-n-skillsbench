# statistical-syllogism

A syllogism that argues, using inductive reasoning, from a generalization true for the most part to a particular case.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates statistical syllogism

## Example

'Almost all people are taller than 26 inches. Gareth is a person. Therefore, Gareth is taller than 26 inches.'
