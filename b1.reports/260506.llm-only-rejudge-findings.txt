REPORT: LLM-ONLY RE-JUDGE OF v1.9 AND v2.0 — SCORES SHIFT UPWARD,
        Δ INCREASES, BENCH SATURATION STILL DIRECTIONAL
================================================================================

Date:   2026-05-06
Status: PARTIAL — v1.9 and v2.0 re-judged under LLM-only path; pre-SFT 4B
        and haiku reference re-judges pending.

================================================================================
QUESTION
================================================================================

The 260506 format-sensitivity finding (b1.reports/260506.bench-format-
sensitivity-finding.txt) showed that the deterministic judge (used for
88.5% of holdout tasks) requires literal "ANSWER:" lines, which pre-SFT
base models do not reliably emit. To enable apples-to-apples
attribution comparison between pre-SFT base models (which need LLM
judge to be measured fairly) and post-SFT students, we ran a full
LLM-only re-judge of v1.9 and v2.0 episodes — using the existing
model responses, just routing them all through the LLM judge
regardless of original query_type.

This report documents the resulting score shifts and what they imply
for the experiment's headline claims.

================================================================================
SETUP
================================================================================

Method     training/rejudge_failed_episodes.py with new flags:
             --all                   target all 400 episodes per run
             --force-llm-judge       override query_type to FREE_FORM
             --out-dir <new>         preserve the originals

Judge      anthropic:claude-opus-4-7 (same as original eval)
Inputs     existing episodes.json files from
             bench-eval-post-sft-v1_9/episodes.json
             bench-eval-post-sft-v2_0/episodes.json
Output     bench-eval-post-sft-v1_9-llm-only/
           bench-eval-post-sft-v2_0-llm-only/

Cost       ~$5 each, ~$10 total. Runs in ~10 min each (no model
           generation; pure judge calls). All 400 episodes per run
           successfully re-judged; zero remaining judge errors.

THREE OBSERVATIONS

  1. BOTH conditions lift under LLM-only judging at BOTH model sizes.
     The deterministic judge was under-counting passes — even SFT'd
     models that were trained to produce ANSWER: format sometimes
     produce conclusions in alternative phrasings ("Final Answer: X",
     "I conclude X", or just stating the answer in prose) that the
     deterministic extractor missed but the LLM judge correctly
     scores.

  2. Curated lifts MORE than baseline under re-judge (ΔCU > ΔBL by
     +2.5 pp at v1.9 and +2.0 pp at v2.0). This means curated-
     condition responses were slightly more likely to be format-
     noncompliant than baseline-condition responses — interpretable
     as: when the procedure block is present, the model occasionally
     embeds its conclusion inside procedural narration rather than
     ending with a literal ANSWER: line. The deterministic judge
     systematically under-counted curated wins.

  3. v2.0 curated is now 0.985 — 197 of 200 tasks passed. The bench
     is essentially solved at 4B + LoRA r=32 + LLM-only scoring.
     v2.0 baseline at 0.920 is also near-ceiling. Whatever residual
     SFT contribution exists at 4B, it is operating in the top
     ~5 pp of headroom on this bench.

================================================================================
SIGNIFICANCE TESTS UNDER LLM-ONLY JUDGE
================================================================================

Per-model McNemar (paired BL vs CU) and bootstrap 95% CI on Δ
(10,000 resamples, paired by task_uid):

                     contingency                  Δ        McNemar       95% CI
v1.9 LLM-only        a=153 b=10 c=30 d=7         +0.100   χ²=9.03,      [+0.040, +0.160]
                                                          p=0.0027
v2.0 LLM-only        a=183 b=1  c=14 d=2         +0.065   exact,        [+0.030, +0.105]
                                                          p=0.00098

Both Δs are now strongly distinguishable from zero:
  - v1.9: p=0.0027 (was p=0.018 under mixed-judge)
  - v2.0: p=0.00098 (was p=0.049 under mixed-judge)

v2.0's Δ now survives Bonferroni correction across the two tests
comfortably (p_adj ≈ 0.002 < 0.025). The earlier worry that v2.0's
Δ was "barely significant" was an artifact of the deterministic
judge under-counting curated wins.

Cross-model bootstrap (paired bootstrap saturating-direction test):
  Δ_v1.9 - Δ_v2.0 = +0.035
  bootstrap 95% CI on the difference: [-0.040, +0.105]
  one-sided p (H₀: Δ_v1.9 ≤ Δ_v2.0): p = 0.191

Saturation result is essentially unchanged from the deterministic-
judge path:
  - mixed-judge:    Δ_v1.9 - Δ_v2.0 = +0.030, p = 0.225
  - LLM-only:       Δ_v1.9 - Δ_v2.0 = +0.035, p = 0.191

The shrinkage of Δ from 2B to 4B is robust to the scoring method —
both judging paths agree on the magnitude (~+0.030 to +0.035) and
on the conclusion that the shrinkage is directional but not
statistically distinguishable at n=200.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================

POSITIVE RESULTS THAT STRENGTHEN

  1. The SFT effect at 2B is more robustly significant than the
     paper currently states. Update v1.9 reporting: Δ +0.100, p=0.003.

  2. The SFT effect at 4B is also robustly significant (no longer
     borderline, no longer Bonferroni-fragile). Update v2.0
     reporting: Δ +0.065, p<0.001.

  3. v2.0 CU 0.985 means the bench is genuinely solved at 4B
     under fair scoring. This is a stronger statement than the
     paper currently makes; the saturation diagnosis is now
     supported by the absolute pass-rate ceiling rather than just
     the Δ shrinkage trend.

CLAIMS THAT ARE UNCHANGED

  4. Bench-saturation comparison still fails the cross-model test
     (p=0.191 one-sided, CI includes 0). Same conclusion as the
     mixed-judge path: directionally consistent with saturation,
     not statistically distinguishable at n=200.

  5. The headline result that v2.0 exceeds the haiku-4-5 reference
     ALSO needs re-checking — haiku's 0.785/0.800 was scored under
     the same deterministic-mixed path and is plausibly under-
     counted by ~5-10 pp. Pending: haiku LLM-only re-judge.

NEW WRINKLE TO ADDRESS

  6. The fact that ΔCU > ΔBL by ~2 pp under re-judge means the
     deterministic judge was systematically biased AGAINST the
     curated condition. This is interpretable: procedure-following
     responses bury their conclusions in narration more often than
     direct-thinking responses do. The bench scoring originally
     under-stated SFT's true contribution.
