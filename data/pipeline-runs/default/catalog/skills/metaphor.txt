# metaphor

a figure of speech that, for rhetorical effect, directly refers to one thing by mentioning another.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates metaphor

## Example

'All the world's a stage, And all the men and women merely players' is a metaphor because it's a comparison without using 'like' or 'as.'
