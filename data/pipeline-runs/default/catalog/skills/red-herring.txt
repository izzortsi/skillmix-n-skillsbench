# red-herring

Introducing irrelevant points to detract attention from a question.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates red herring

## Example

A member of the press asks the president why they voted to expand a welfare program. The president responds, 'The strength of America is the strength of its communities, and I am proud to make our communities better places.'
