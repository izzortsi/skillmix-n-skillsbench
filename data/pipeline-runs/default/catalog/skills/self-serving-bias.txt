# self-serving-bias

A cognitive or perceptual process that is distorted by the need to maintain and enhance one's self esteem.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates self serving bias

## Example

If I do well on the exam, it's because of my academic prowess and hard work. If I do poorly, it's because the course was poorly taught, and the exam was poorly proctored.
