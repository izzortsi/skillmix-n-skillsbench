# modus-ponens

A syllogism that is of the form 'If P then Q. P. Hence Q.'

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates modus ponens

## Example

'If today is Tuesday, then John will go to work. Today is Tuesday. Therefore, John will go to work.'
