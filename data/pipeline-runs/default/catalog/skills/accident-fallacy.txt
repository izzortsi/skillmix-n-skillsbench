# accident-fallacy

an informal fallacy and a deductively valid but unsound argument occurring in a statistical syllogism (an argument based on a generalization) when an exception to a rule of thumb is ignored.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates accident (fallacy)

## Example

Cutting people with knives is a crime. Surgeons cut people with knives. Surgeons are criminals.
