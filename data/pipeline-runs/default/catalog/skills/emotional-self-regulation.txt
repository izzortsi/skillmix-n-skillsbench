# emotional-self-regulation

a complex process that involves initiating, inhibiting, or modulating one's state or behavior in a given situation.

## When to Use

- producing text that illustrates emotional self regulation

## Example

Examples of emotional self regulation include meditating, pausing to collect oneself before speaking, and practicing stress management.
